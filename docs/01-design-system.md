# Omvana Design System

Version: 1.0

---

## Design Philosophy

The website should feel peaceful, luxurious, warm, and minimal.

It should never feel like a template or a corporate website.

Every page should breathe through generous spacing, elegant typography, soft colours, and immersive photography.

Design inspiration:

- Omvana Yoga Retreat
- Aman Resorts
- Six Senses
- Apple
- Notion

---

## Principles

1. Simplicity over decoration.

2. Large whitespace.

3. Calm visual hierarchy.

4. Beautiful readable typography.

5. Editorial layout.

6. Large photography.

7. Minimal borders.

8. Soft rounded corners.

9. Smooth animations.

10. Premium user experience.

---

## UI Personality

Elegant

Luxury

Peaceful

Minimal

Natural

Warm

Editorial

Slow

Organic

Timeless

Never flashy.

Never colourful.

Never use gradients unless specifically required.

Avoid excessive shadows.

Avoid glassmorphism.

Avoid neumorphism.

Avoid overly modern AI-looking dashboards.

---

## Overall Feel

Imagine someone opening the website after a stressful day.

The website itself should feel relaxing.

The interface should disappear behind the content.
